Volunteer Management System Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

This policy explains how the ISKCON of New Jersey Volunteer Management System (the “Application”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information about volunteers, administrators, and owners.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address, telephone number, and account identifiers.
  • Skills, availability, participation status, and notification preferences.
  • Task assignments, event participation, check-in records, service hours, and feedback.
  • Planning information entered by authorized administrators.
  • Login and security audit information, including date, time, role, browser, platform, and timezone.
  • Notification delivery records, including accepted, sent, delivered, read, and failed statuses when supplied by a provider.

How we use information

  • Register and authenticate users and administer access.
  • Plan events, staff volunteer tasks, and coordinate service.
  • Send requested task reminders and operational communications by WhatsApp, email, or browser notification.
  • Record service, improve planning, troubleshoot delivery, secure the Application, and maintain audit history.
  • Prepare operational reports for authorized temple leadership.

WhatsApp communications

WhatsApp reminders are sent when a telephone number and applicable notification preference or consent are recorded. Messages may include a volunteer’s first name and an assigned task’s title, date, time, and location. Delivery is processed through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Volunteers can change their reminder preference in their profile or contact us for assistance.

How information is shared

Information is available to authorized users according to role. We use service providers needed to operate the Application, including Google Firebase for authentication, database, hosting, and server functions; Meta for WhatsApp Business messages; and configured email or browser-notification providers. We do not sell personal information or use volunteer information for third-party advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users, the temple, or the Application.

Retention and deletion

We retain information as reasonably needed for volunteer coordination, service history, security, reporting, legal obligations, and dispute prevention. Some deleted operational records may remain temporarily recoverable, and limited historical or audit information may be retained where necessary. To request access, correction, or deletion, email us below. We may verify the requester’s identity and retain information when required or permitted by law.

Security and children

We use role-based access controls and reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security. The Application is intended for authorized temple volunteers and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

Users may review and update available profile fields and notification preferences. Users may request help correcting information, stopping WhatsApp reminders, or deleting an account.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the Application or our practices change. The effective date will be revised when a material update is published.
